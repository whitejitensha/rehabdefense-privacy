RehabDefense — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

Overview

RehabDefense is a clinical documentation reference application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Data Collection

RehabDefense does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, health information, or usage analytics through its own systems. All app data — including favorites, custom snippets, and preferences — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Advertising

The free version of RehabDefense displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and general device information to serve relevant ads. This data is handled by Google according to their Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads. Upgrading to RehabDefense Premium removes all advertisements and associated data collection.

Data Storage

All data created within the app — including favorites, custom snippets, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only. This data is not accessible to anyone other than you.

Third-Party Services

RehabDefense uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and general device information to serve ads. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services are used. The app loads a web font (Google Fonts) for display purposes.

Health Information

RehabDefense is a clinical documentation reference tool. It does not collect, process, or store any Protected Health Information (PHI), patient data, or personal health information. The app contains only pre-written template language for clinical documentation purposes.

Children's Privacy

RehabDefense is designed for healthcare professionals and is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.

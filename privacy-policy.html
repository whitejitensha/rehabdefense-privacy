RehabDefense — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

Overview

RehabDefense is a clinical documentation reference application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Data Collection

RehabDefense does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, analytics, or health information. The app operates entirely on your device with no server communication.

Data Storage

All data created within the app — including favorites, custom snippets, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

Third-Party Services

RehabDefense does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services. The app loads a web font (Google Fonts) for display purposes; this is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Health Information

RehabDefense is a clinical documentation reference tool. It does not collect, process, or store any Protected Health Information (PHI), patient data, or personal health information. The app contains only pre-written template language for clinical documentation purposes.

Children's Privacy

RehabDefense is designed for healthcare professionals and is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
